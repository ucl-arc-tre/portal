## Email sent to IAO/IAA once Study/Project has been moved

Dear <IAO/IAA name>, ​

The records pertaining to your Study <study name> have been moved to the [ARC Services Portal](linky). Your access to the records on SharePoint has been revoked. ​

Please log into the [Portal](https://portal.arc.ucl.ac.uk/​) to complete your profile and check the details of your Study are correct. If something is not correct, please get in touch via [MyServices](https://myservices.ucl.ac.uk/self-service/requests/new/select_template?from=wizard&service_id=2616&service_instance_id=6065) to let us know.

Note that if your training has expired you will need to renew it in order to retain access to any Environments (i.e TRE/DSH) you use. Training Certificates can be uploaded to your [Profile](https://portal.arc.ucl.ac.uk/profile) on the Portal.

​

Please get in touch with the Portal team via [MyServices](https://myservices.ucl.ac.uk/self-service/requests/new/select_template?from=wizard&service_id=2616&service_instance_id=6065) if you need any help logging in to the Portal or if something is does not look right once you've log in.

​

Kind Regards,

ARC Services Portal Team​
